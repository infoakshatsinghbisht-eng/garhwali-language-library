Title: धारी देवी जागर - हे देवी कल्याणी (Dhari Devi Jagar - Hey Devi Kalyani)
Artist/Origin: Pritam Bhartwan / Traditional Jagar
Genre: Sacred Jagar / Divine Invocation
Theme: Invocation of Goddess Kali at the rock sanctuary of Dhari Devi

Lyrics:
उठो माता कल्याणी, धारी की भवानी!
अलकनंदा का बीच धारा, तेरो छ मंडाण!
डौल-दमाऊँ बाजे, रणसिंघा को नाद,
रक्षा कर माता, तेरो सेवक पुकार!
सिद्धपीठ धारी मा दीप जलो भारी,
पापियों को संहार कर, भक्तां तैं उबारी!
जय माँ धारी देवी, जय माँ कल्याणी!

English Translation:
Awaken O benevolent Mother, Bhavani of Dhari! Amidst the surging torrent of the Alaknanda river stands Thy holy sanctum!

Cultural Context:
Dhari Devi is the guardian deity of Uttarakhand whose deity icon changes form from girl in morning to woman at noon and elder at dusk.
