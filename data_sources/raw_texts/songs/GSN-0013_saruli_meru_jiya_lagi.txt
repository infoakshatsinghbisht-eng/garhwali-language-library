Title: सरुली मेरु जिया लागी (Saruli Meru Jiya Lagi)
Artist/Origin: Traditional Courtship Song
Genre: Jhumelo / Romance
Theme: Tender romantic banter across terraced fields and springs

Lyrics:
सरुली, मेरु जिया लागी तेरो मुखड़ा मा!
घास काटदी तू डांडा मा, रूप चमके धूप मा!
ऐ जा बांद, हाथ जोड़ि ब्वोल,
कख तेरो गौं, कख तेरो थान?
घस्यारी बांद मेरी, हंसदी खिल-खिल,
तेरी सुरमई आंख्युं मा मेरो बसे दिल!

English Translation:
O Saruli, my heart has become enchanted by your lovely face! As you cut grass upon the terraced ridges, your grace shines in the sun!

Cultural Context:
Celebrates the joyful spirit of mountain maidens (Ghyasari) harvesting fodder across emerald hillsides while exchanging lyrical wit.
