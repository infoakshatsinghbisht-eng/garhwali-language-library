Title: इगास बग्वाल - भैलो खेलो रे भैलो (Igas Bagwal - Bhalo Khelo Re Bhalo)
Artist/Origin: Traditional Festival Song
Genre: Festival Folk Dance
Theme: Celebrating the return of hero Madho Singh Bhandari eleven days after Diwali

Lyrics:
भैलो रे भैलो, इगास को भैलो!
माधो सिंह भंडारी जितिक ऐगे,
चीड़ की छिल्ला मा आग जलाई,
घरे-घरे पूड़ी अर रोट पकाई!
गौं-गौं मा नाचां, ढोल बजावां,
बीर भड़ों की कीर्ति गावां!
इगास बग्वाल को शगुन मनावां!

English Translation:
Bhalo, O Bhalo! The sacred spin-torch of Igas! Madho Singh Bhandari returned victorious from Tibet, light pine torches and fry the festive breads!

Cultural Context:
Igas Bagwal is celebrated 11 days after Diwali in Garhwal because news of Lord Rama and hero Madho Singh's victories reached the mountains late.
