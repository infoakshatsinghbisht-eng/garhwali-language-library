TITLE: छ्वि मण को (Chhwi Manko) (Chhwi Manko)
ID: GSN-0021
ARTIST: Narendra Singh Negi
GENRE: Romantic Folk Lyrical / Ballad
CORPUS: Contemporary Classic Garhwali Poetry
THEME: Heartfelt unspoken love, mountain gazes, and romantic longing
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/narendra_singh_negi_classics

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
One of Narendra Singh Negi's immortal melodies depicting the bashful romantic encounters of village youth along mountain trails.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
छ्वि मण को मण मा ही रैगे, बोलि न सके मेरी छैला!
आँखी-आँखी मा बात ह्वेगे, खोलि न सके मेरी छैला!
धारों मा बुरांश फूली, मन मा उमंग छैला!
घस्यारी बणि डांडी गै, घाणि पाणी भरि ल्याई!
बाटा मा नजर मिली, रूप देखि हिय हरषाई!
छ्वि मण को मण मा ही रैगे, बोलि न सके मेरी छैला!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
The whisper of the heart remained within the heart, unable to be spoken, O my beloved! Through eyes alone words were exchanged, yet secrets could not be unveiled!
