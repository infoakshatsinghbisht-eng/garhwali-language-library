Title: फूलदेई छम छम - फूल संक्रांति (Phooldei Chham Chham - Phool Sankranti)
Artist/Origin: Traditional Children's Spring Song
Genre: Spring Festival Ballad
Theme: Greeting the first day of Chaitra with spring blossoms placed upon thresholds

Lyrics:
फूलदेई, छम्मा देई,
दैणी द्वार, भर भकार!
यो देहली सकार, बारम्बार नमस्कार!
फूले फूल बुरांश का, लाये थाल सजाय!
घर-घर जावां, फूल बिखरावां,
चावल अर गुड़ को उपहार पावां!
सदा रहे सुख-समृद्धि, फले-फूले संसार!

English Translation:
Phooldei, Chhama Dei! May your doors remain blessed, may your granaries overflow with golden harvest! We honor this sacred doorstep with mountain flowers!

Cultural Context:
On the first day of Chaitra, children wake at dawn, gather flowers of Buransh, Panya, and mustard, and place them on every village doorstep.
