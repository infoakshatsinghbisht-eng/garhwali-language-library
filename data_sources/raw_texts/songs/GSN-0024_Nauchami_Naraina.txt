TITLE: नौचमी नरैण (Nauchami Naraina) (Nauchami Naraina)
ID: GSN-0024
ARTIST: Narendra Singh Negi
GENRE: Social & Political Satire / Folk Protest
CORPUS: Uttarakhand Social Consciousness Poetry
THEME: Governance critique, political hypocrisy, and cultural defense
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/nauchami_naraina_protest

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
The most historic sociopolitical song in Uttarakhand modern history, which galvanized public integrity and political accountability across the hills.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
नौचमी नरैण, तू त बड़ो छली रै!
पहाड़ा का भोळा लोकों ते, कुकर्म सिखै गै!
दस हाथ की धोती पहरी, मुंड मा मुकुट सजायो!
गाँव-गाँव मा बांटी दारू, नेता जी बणि आयो!
देवभूमि की लाज बचावा, उठो हे नौनिहालो!
नौचमी नरैण, तू त बड़ो छली रै!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
O nine-faced Narayana, you are truly a master of deceit! You have deceived the innocent mountain folk, donning a fine ten-yard dhoti while acting as a virtuous politician!
