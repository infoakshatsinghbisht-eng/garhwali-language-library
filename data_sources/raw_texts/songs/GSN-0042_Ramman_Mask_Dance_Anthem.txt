TITLE: रम्माण मुखौटा नृत्य गीत (Ramman Mask Dance Anthem) (Ramman Mask Dance Anthem)
ID: GSN-0042
ARTIST: Saloor Dungra Villagers (UNESCO Intangible Heritage)
GENRE: Ritual Theatre / Mask Dance Chant
CORPUS: Chamoli Religious Performance Traditions
THEME: Lord Bhumiyal invocation, mythological masks (Narsingh, Ram, Kuror), and epic victory
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/unesco_ramman_heritage

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, Ramman is a sacred religious theatre practiced in the Painkhanda region.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
रम्माण आयो रे सलूर डूंगरा मा, भूमियाल देव बिराजे!
काष्ठ का मुखौटा पहरी, ढोल-दमाऊँ बाजे!
नरसिंह का नृत्य मा, पापी थर-थर कांपे!
कुरुड़ पंछी आयो, माल मल्ल युद्ध ह्वेगे!
विश्व धरोहर हमरी, संस्कृति अमर रैगे!
रम्माण आयो रे सलूर डूंगरा मा, भूमियाल देव बिराजे!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
The festival of Ramman has arrived in Saloor Dungra village, where Lord Bhumiyal reigns! Wearing carved wooden masks to resonating drums, Lord Narsingh dances triumphantly!
