TITLE: नरसिंह देवता जागर (Narsingh Devta Jagar) (Narsingh Devta Jagar)
ID: GSN-0029
ARTIST: Traditional Jagariya Heritage
GENRE: Jagar / Tantric-Pahari Invocation
CORPUS: Garhwal Village Guardian Rituals
THEME: Fierce protective avatar, destruction of malevolence, and domestic safety
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/narsingh_jagar_garhwal

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Narsingh is installed in almost every traditional Garhwali household alter (Thaan) as the ultimate sovereign against misfortune.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
नरसिंह अवतार हे बीर, खंभ फाड़िक प्रकट ह्वेया!
ह्रिम-ह्रिम गर्जना करा, दुष्टों को नाश करा!
धुंयाधार छानी मा, रक्षा का दीप जलाया!
भैरों-काली संग नाचा, न्याय को मार्ग दिखाया!
देवभूमि की माटी मा, सत्य को दीप जलाया!
नरसिंह अवतार हे बीर, खंभ फाड़िक प्रकट ह्वेया!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
O fierce Lord Narsingh Veer, bursting forth from the stone pillar with thunderous roar! Annihilate evil forces and ignite the eternal lamp of protection across our hearths!
