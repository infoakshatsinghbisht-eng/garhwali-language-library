TITLE: फ्यूंली फूलिगे धारों मा (Phyooli Phoolige Dharon Ma) (Phyooli Phoolige Dharon Ma)
ID: GSN-0044
ARTIST: Traditional Spring Vernal Singers
GENRE: Ritu-Geet / Spring Vernal Lyric
CORPUS: Central Pahari Nature Folklore
THEME: Yellow blossom of Phyooli flower, mountain spring rebirth, and tender folk legend
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/phyooli_spring_ballads

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Phyooli (Reinwardtia indica) is Uttarakhand's iconic golden wildflower, immortalized in folk tales as a fairy maiden of the woods.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
फ्यूंली फूलिगे धारों मा, पीली चादर बिछी गै!
पहाड़ा का हर आंगण मा, बासंती बयार ऐगै!
नानी बांद हँसणी छन, मन मा उमंग छैला!
बण-उपवन मा पक्षी गांदा, खुशियों को संदेशा ल्यांदा!
फ्यूंली की कोमलता जनि, पवित्र च मेरु गढ़वाल!
फ्यूंली फूलिगे धारों मा, पीली चादर बिछी गै!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
The delicate yellow Phyooli has blossomed upon the mountain ridges, draping a golden cloak across the hills! The vernal spring breeze enters every courtyard with joyful news!
