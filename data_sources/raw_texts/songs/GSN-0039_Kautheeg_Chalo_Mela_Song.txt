TITLE: कौथीग चलो - जौनसार-गढ़वाल मेला (Kautheeg Chalo) (Kautheeg Chalo Mela Song)
ID: GSN-0039
ARTIST: Traditional Mela Performers
GENRE: Fair / Festive Community Song
CORPUS: Garhwal Fair & Festival Folklore
THEME: Annual village fair excitement, colorful attire, jalebi sweets, and reunion
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/garhwal_kautheeg_mela

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Kautheeg fairs (like Gauchar, Srinagar Vaikunth Chaturdashi, Uttarkashi Magh Mela) are the epicenters of social life in Garhwal.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
कौथीग चलो रे साथी, गौं को मेलो लागो!
झूला झूलो रे नौनू, मन को हर्ष जागो!
रंग-बिरंगी पिछौड़ा पहरी, बांद सज-धज आई!
जलेबी-पकौड़ी की दुकान, खिलौना का हाट!
ढोल-दमाऊँ की गूंज मा, नाचे पूरा गाँव!
कौथीग चलो रे साथी, गौं को मेलो लागो!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
Come let us go to the mountain fair, the annual village festival has arrived! Children swing upon great wooden rides, while maidens arrive in resplendent attire!
