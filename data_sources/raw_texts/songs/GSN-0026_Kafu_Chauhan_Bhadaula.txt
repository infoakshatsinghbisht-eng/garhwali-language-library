TITLE: काफू चौहान भड़ौला (Kafu Chauhan Pawada) (Kafu Chauhan Bhadaula)
ID: GSN-0026
ARTIST: Traditional Bardic Oral Epic (Dhaki/Auji Heritage)
GENRE: Pawada / Historical Martial Epic
CORPUS: Garhwal 52 Garh Chieftain Lore
THEME: Heroic valor, fortress resistance, and pride of Uppugarh
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/garhwal_pawada_epics

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
The ultimate epic ballad of Garhwali indomitable independence and chivalry celebrating Chieftain Kafu Chauhan of Uppugarh fort.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
उप्पुगढ़ को काफू चौहान, रण मा ललकार्यो!
तिन सिरी कटि सकद, पण झुक नि सकद!
अजयपाल की सेना आगू, सिंह जनि गर्ज्यो!
तलवार की धार मा, धर्म निभाया!
बावन गढ़ मा अमर नाम, वीरत्व दिखाया!
उप्पुगढ़ को काफू चौहान, रण मा ललकार्यो!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
Kafu Chauhan of Uppugarh roared upon the battlefield! 'My head may be severed in three pieces, but it shall never bow!' Like a lion he stood before King Ajay Pal's marching legion!
