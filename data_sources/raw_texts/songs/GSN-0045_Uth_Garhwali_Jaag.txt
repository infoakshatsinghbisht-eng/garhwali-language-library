TITLE: उठ गढ़वाली जाग (Uth Garhwali Jaag) (Uth Garhwali Jaag)
ID: GSN-0045
ARTIST: Chandra Kunwar Bartwal & Modern Bards
GENRE: Nationalistic / Awakening Anthem
CORPUS: Pahari Renaissance Poetry
THEME: Language pride, Himalayan ecology conservation, and cultural awakening
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/garhwal_renaissance_anthems

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
The anthem of the Garhwali linguistic and cultural movement, reminding newer generations to speak, write, and treasure their heritage.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
उठ गढ़वाली जाग रे, अपणी बोली-भाषा बचा!
हिमालय का शिखरों बटी, स्वाभिमान को बिगुल बजा!
माटी तेरी देवभूमि, संस्कृति तेरी पहचान!
जंगलों की रक्षा करा, नदियां का मान बचा!
गढ़वाली भाषा अमर हो, गर्व से तू सिर उठा!
उठ गढ़वाली जाग रे, अपणी बोली-भाषा बचा!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
Arise and awaken, O son of Garhwal! Preserve your mother tongue and living heritage! Sound the trumpet of dignity from Himalayan heights; this soil is your sanctum!
