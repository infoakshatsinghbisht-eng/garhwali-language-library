TITLE: सेम मुखेम नागराजा जागर (Sem Mukhem Nagaraja Jagar) (Sem Mukhem Nagaraja Jagar)
ID: GSN-0030
ARTIST: Traditional Tehri Bards
GENRE: Jagar / Serpent-Krishna Ballad
CORPUS: Tehri Garhwal Sacred Geographies
THEME: Sem Mukhem shrine pilgrimage, Krishna avatar, and emerald pine groves
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/sem_mukhem_nagaraja

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Sem Mukhem in Tehri Garhwal is one of the holiest shrines of Nagaraja where thousands trek through oak forests seeking healing and offspring.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
जय सेम मुखेम का नागराजा, फणीधर स्वामी महाराज!
मुखेम का डांडा मा, बंसी बाजे दिन-रात!
कन्हैया बणि बिराजा, नाग रूप धारी!
दूध-मलीदा चढ़ावा, पाप-दोष निवारण करा!
जो भी तेरी शरण ऐगे, ताको बेड़ा पार करा!
जय सेम मुखेम का नागराजा, फणीधर स्वामी महाराज!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
Hail to the Serpent King of Sem Mukhem, the crowned sovereign Lord! Upon the heights of Mukhem hill, flute melodies echo day and night as Lord Krishna in serpentine splendour!
