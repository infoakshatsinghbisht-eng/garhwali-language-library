Title: छोरी गढ़वाली - बांद मेरी छैला (Chhori Garhwali - Band Meri Chhaila)
Artist/Origin: Traditional Festive Dance
Genre: Chhori / Group Dance
Theme: Energetic syncopated dance at autumn village harvest fairs

Lyrics:
छोरी रे छोरी, गढ़वाल का डांडा मा!
कमर मा घाघरी, नाक मा टिहरी नथुली,
झुमिक-झुमिक नाचे जब कौथिग मा,
सारा संसार देखदो छैला!
ढोल को डंका जब बाजे गम्भीर,
पांव मा घुंघरू छनके वीर!
जय गढ़वाल, जय देवभूमि!

English Translation:
O Garhwali maiden on the high ridges! Swirling in your traditional skirt and golden Tehri nose ring, dancing at the mountain fair!

Cultural Context:
Chhori is the lively circular folk dance performed with interlocking arms during autumn village fairs (Kauthig).
