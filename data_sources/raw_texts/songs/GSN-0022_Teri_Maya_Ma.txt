TITLE: तेरी मया मा (Teri Maya Ma) (Teri Maya Ma)
ID: GSN-0022
ARTIST: Narendra Singh Negi & Anuradha Nirala
GENRE: Romantic Duet / Folk Lyric
CORPUS: Central Himalayan Love Ballads
THEME: Unconditional devotion, mutual trust, and shared mountain dreams
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/garhwali_romantic_duets

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
The pinnacle of Garhwali duet songwriting, celebrated throughout Uttarakhand as a masterwork of pastoral romantic poetry.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
तेरी मया मा ह्वेगे मि बावरो, डांडा-कांठा घूम्दू छौं!
तू च मेरी हंसुली, तू च मेरु झुमका, त्वेते मन मा ध्यांदू छौं!
पहाड़ा की ठंडी बयार, मन मा भरिगे प्यार!
रूपा की बांद तू सुण, म्यारा हिया की धड़कन!
गंगा जी की धार जनि, निर्मल च तेरु मन!
तेरी मया मा ह्वेगे मि बावरो, डांडा-कांठा घूम्दू छौं!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
In your divine love I have turned mad, wandering across mountain ridges! You are my hansuli necklace, you are my jhumka earring, I meditate upon you in my soul!
