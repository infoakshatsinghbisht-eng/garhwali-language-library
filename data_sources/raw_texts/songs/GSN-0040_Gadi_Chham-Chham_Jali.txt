TITLE: गाड़ी छम-छम जाली - मोटर बाटा (Gadi Chham Chham Jali) (Gadi Chham-Chham Jali)
ID: GSN-0040
ARTIST: Narendra Singh Negi
GENRE: Modern Folk Transition Song / Travel Ballad
CORPUS: Contemporary Himalayan Social History
THEME: Arrival of motor roads, travel to pilgrimage shrines, and changing village lifestyles
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/gadi_chham_chham_jali

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Chronicles the dramatic transition from walking mountain trails to the advent of motor roads connecting pilgrimage hubs.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
गाड़ी छम-छम जाली मोटर बाटा मा,
कोटद्वार बटी श्रीनगर पौड़ी का घाटा मा!
ड्राइवर भैजी धीरे चला, घुमावदार मोड़ छन!
खिड़कियों बटी दिखनी अलकनंदा की धार,
जय बदरी-केदार बोलिक चलिगे सवार!
गाड़ी छम-छम जाली मोटर बाटा मा!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
The bus rolls smoothly along the mountain motor road, winding from Kotdwar to Srinagar through the ridges of Pauri! O driver brother, drive gently along these curving bends!
