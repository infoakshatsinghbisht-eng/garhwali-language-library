TITLE: हिलजात्रा लखिया भूत गीत (Hilljatra Lakhiya Bhoot Ballad) (Hilljatra Lakhiya Bhoot Ballad)
ID: GSN-0043
ARTIST: Traditional Pastoral Balladeers
GENRE: Carnival / Pastoral Mask Ballad
CORPUS: Himalayan Agro-Pastoral Festival Chants
THEME: Monsoon paddy transplantation, Lakhiya Bhoot dancer, bullocks and farming blessings
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/hilljatra_pastoral_chants

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Celebrates the agrarian life, teamwork, bullock ploughing, and divine protection during the arduous monsoon paddy transplantation.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
हिलजात्रा आई रे भाई, रोपणी को त्योहार!
लखिया भूत आयो रण मा, महादेव को गण अपार!
सफेद बल्डों की जोड़ी, खेतों मा जोती गै!
धान की रोपणी ह्वेली, हरी-भरी फसल लहराली!
देवता को आशीष ल्ह्याया, गाँव-गौं सुख-शांति होली!
हिलजात्रा आई रे भाई, रोपणी को त्योहार!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
The pastoral carnival of Hilljatra has arrived, festival of monsoon paddy transplanting! Lakhiya Bhoot, fierce attendant of Shiva, enters the muddy arena with decorated white bullocks!
