TITLE: स्याली स्वरूपा (Syali Swarupa) (Syali Swarupa)
ID: GSN-0025
ARTIST: Narendra Singh Negi
GENRE: Humorous Folk Song / Kinship Banter
CORPUS: Central Pahari Kinship Ballads
THEME: Sister-in-law (syali) teasing, marriage celebrations, and family laughter
SOURCE: https://archive.org/details/garhwali_kinship_songs

=== CULTURAL CONTEXT ===
Captures the playful traditional banter between brother-in-law (Bhina) and sister-in-law (Syali) typical of Garhwali weddings.

=== DEVANGARI LYRICS ===
स्याली स्वरूपा, नाक की नथुली चमकी!
भिना जी का घर ऐगे, पायल की झनकार खनकी!
मीठी बोली बोलिक, हिया हरषाई!
दमाऊँ की थाप मा, झुमेलो लगाई!
पहाड़ी रस्म-रिवाज मा, हास्य-रस समाई!
स्याली स्वरूपा, नाक की नथुली चमकी!

=== ENGLISH TRANSLATION ===
O graceful sister-in-law Swarupa, how brilliantly shines your golden nath nose-ring! Arriving at brother-in-law's home with tinkling anklets!
